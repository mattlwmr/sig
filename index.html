Installation Instructions

Apple Mail

  1. Fill in your details on the left
  2. Click inside the Live Preview on the right
  3. Press Cmd+A then Cmd+C to copy it
  4. Open Mail → Settings → Signatures
  5. Click + to create a new signature
  6. Click in the signature field and press Cmd+V

Gmail

  1. Fill in your details on the left
  2. Click inside the Live Preview on the right
  3. Press Cmd+A then Cmd+C to copy it
  4. In Gmail go to Settings → See all settings → General
  5. Scroll to Signature and click Create new
  6. Click inside the signature box and press Cmd+V
  7. Click Save Changes

Outlook (Mac & Windows)

  1. Fill in your details on the left
  2. Click inside the Live Preview on the right
  3. Press Ctrl/Cmd+A then Ctrl/Cmd+C to copy it
  4. In Outlook go to File → Options → Mail → Signatures (Windows) or Outlook → Settings → Signatures (Mac)
  5. Click New and give the signature a name
  6. Click inside the signature field and press Ctrl/Cmd+V
  7. Click OK / Save

Rackspace Webmail

  1. Fill in your details on the left
  2. Click inside the Live Preview on the right
  3. Press Ctrl/Cmd+A then Ctrl/Cmd+C to copy it
  4. Log into apps.rackspace.com
  5. Click the gear icon → Settings
  6. Go to Composing Email → Signatures
  7. Click Add Signature and give it a name
  8. Switch editor to Rich Text / HTML mode
  9. Click inside the field and press Ctrl/Cmd+V
  10. Click Save
